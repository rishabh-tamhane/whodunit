## Moderator script

1. Ask the Game Master to create/configure the room without coaching. Observe
   whether the recovery-link and non-playing-host boundaries are understood.
2. Ask every player to join, identify their slot, request approval, acknowledge
   fictional use, and complete or decline every private description.
3. Observe dossier comprehension and ask participants not to read private
   screens aloud unless the game directs them to.
4. Let the Game Master pace rounds and missions. Record conversation balance,
   mission achievability, evidence comprehension, and moments when the site
   distracts from in-person play.
5. Use at least one stalled-discussion intervention. In the stress session,
   force a provider timeout so the prepared fallback path is exercised.
6. In the stress session, disconnect/reconnect one player device and recover
   the Game Master in a clean browser; verify old devices lose authority.
7. Submit an early suspicion and confirm it neither eliminates a player nor
   reveals correctness. Complete final cases, reveal, scores, and case file.
8. Debrief separately with the Game Master and players before discussing the
   implementation.

## Debrief prompts

- What did you believe the site knew about you, and what did the Game Master
  know?
- When did the site help the conversation, and when did it interrupt it?
- Which mission or clue felt clearest, least fair, or most fun?
- Did the early suspicion and final accusation feel meaningfully different?
- Did the reveal explain the motive, method, timeline, evidence, and faction
  roles without contradiction?
- Would you confidently run/join another game without a developer present?
